Let’s Pray

Privacy

Let’s Pray is a personal prayer list. We do not collect, see, sell or share anything you put in it. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Where your prayers are kept

Everything you add stays on your own device.

Sync between your devices

If you turn on sync, a copy is kept in a gist in your own GitHub account, using a token you create. Before it leaves your device it is encrypted (AES-256-GCM) with a key made from a passphrase only you know. Your passphrase is never stored or sent anywhere, and neither GitHub nor we can read your prayers. You can turn sync off at any time.

Reminders

Daily and follow-up reminders are scheduled on your iPhone. They are not sent through any server. Unless you choose to show details, reminders only say how many prayer points you have.

Backups

Backups you export are locked with a passphrase unless you choose otherwise, and go only where you save or send them.

Questions

Ask through the contact details on the App Store page for Let’s Pray.
