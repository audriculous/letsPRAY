Let’s Pray

Privacy

Let’s Pray is a personal prayer list. We do not collect, see, sell or share anything you put in it. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Where your prayers are kept

Everything you add stays on your own device.

Sync between your devices

If you turn on sync, a copy is kept in a gist in your own GitHub account, using a token you create. Before it leaves your device it is encrypted (AES-256-GCM) with a key made from a passphrase only you know. Your passphrase is never stored or sent anywhere, and neither GitHub nor we can read your prayers. You can turn sync off at any time.

Shared lists

If you share a list, it is kept in your own iCloud account and shared only with the people you invite, using Apple’s iCloud sharing. Prayer details are stored in iCloud’s end-to-end encrypted fields, so only you and the people you invite can read them. When you last prayed, your pins and follow-ups are never shared. You can stop sharing or leave a shared list at any time.

Devotion journal and photos

Journal entries and reading ticks stay on your device, and sync only if you turn sync on (encrypted, as above). Photos of journal pages are never synced or uploaded; they stay on the device that took them, and in backups you export. Reading the text in a photo happens on your device: in the iPhone app with Apple’s built-in text recognition, and on the web with a text reader that runs in your browser. The web reader’s program files are downloaded from jsDelivr the first time you use it; your photo is not sent with them. Bible readings open on Bible Gateway’s website, which has its own privacy policy.

Reminders

Daily and follow-up reminders are scheduled on your iPhone. They are not sent through any server. Unless you choose to show details, reminders only say how many prayer points you have.

Backups

Backups you export are locked with a passphrase unless you choose otherwise, and go only where you save or send them.
